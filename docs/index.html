How it works

Rule-based composite

A deterministic classifier combining three diagnostics from classic technical statistics: trend (EMA 21/50/200 stack + rolling log-price regression), volatility (ATR% and a Bollinger-vs-Keltner squeeze), and chop (Wilder ADX). Fully explainable — no trained model.

Statistical HMM

A 4-state Gaussian Hidden Markov Model over a standardized, PCA-whitened feature vector built entirely from public price data — SPY trend spreads, VIX percentile & slope, sector-ETF breadth, credit spread, copper/gold, and yield-curve proxies. Reports a Bayesian posterior over the four regimes.

Not investment advice. This is a research / portfolio project that describes the market's current state, not what to buy or sell. The HMM is a fixed snapshot trained on ~2022–2026 data and is not continuously retrained.
